    OBD-II and EV diagnostics

    Vehicle speed uses a virtual OBD-II reply. The remaining signals come from the EV model.

    Speed request 01 0D (service 1, PID 13 in decimal) Simulated reply — [9]

    The bytes are hexadecimal. The last reply byte gives speed in km/h.

      Reading energy consumption

      The simulator reports DC energy at the pack terminals. Discharge minus regeneration, divided by distance, gives net pack consumption. 1 kWh/100 km = 10 Wh/km. The live value appears after 10 m and can be negative on a downhill run.

      WLTP reports electric energy consumption in Wh/km [5]. The 2020 U.S. SR+ label gives 24 kWh/100 mi (about 14.9 kWh/100 km) [3]. The U.S. procedure includes AC recharge energy [4].
